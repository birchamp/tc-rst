Make or break a verse span

Join two or more verses into one span, such as 2-3, when your language needs to reorder their content. Break the span again the same way. Bible projects only.

A verse span (also called a verse bridge) is two or more verses translated as one unit, for example 2-3.

Steps

  1. Open the section with its Draft section {span} label, type the text, and click Place verse numbers.
  2. Pick up the later number, for example 3.
  3. Click the word where the earlier verse (2) begins. The tooltip says "Join verse 3 to verse 2 at …".
  1. Click Save section. The verses are saved as one span, numbered 2-3.

Break a span

Open the section again, click Place verse numbers, pick up the 3 and click the word where verse 3 should begin. Then click Save section.

Good to know

  • In Type mode you can also start a line with the span, for example 2-3 ….
  • Making or breaking a span marks the checks and alignments of those verses for review. Nothing is deleted. See Editing a verse you already checked or aligned.
  • Spans can't be made in Verse view.
  • Open Bible Stories have frames, not verse numbers, so they have no spans.
