# S3: Simplify the store before sync

## Goal

After S2 merges unchanged, the store gets one simplification pass before sync (X) adds writers:
every preservation copy goes through one no-replace helper; one pure `journal_file::next` decides
every journal file's state; the three longest executors are split by step; the ledger's facts
are pure; a derived path's state lives in the ledger, not in the Writer's maps.

Then step 9 applies the owner's rulings of 2026-10-21 (`docs/STORAGE-DELTA-2026-10-21.md`). It
deletes automatic recording of outside edits, the `metadata.json` recording, R10 with
`Who::Ambiguous`, and the carried backups. It adds the `kept` list, the held-waiting state,
*Import*, *Not now* and *Bring it back* in the store, reusing the planner. Step 9 is no longer
optional. C9 (content-addressed carried versions) is gone.

## Why it matters to a translator

A translator never sees it. It protects their saves: 9 of 10 storage fixes since the ledger
landed were in the code around the pure machine (health-storage F3), and 19 of S2's 40 loss rows
need another program to cut or delete a journal file first; journal files have no state machine
(F11, an inference from STORAGE §15.5).

After step 9 a translator sees it. A book file changed in Paratext is kept, and the app asks
"This book has been changed externally. Would you like to import it into this project?" Nothing
enters the project unless they choose *Import*. A changed settings file is written again, with a
kept copy and a one-line notice. A crash of the app's own write never asks a question.

## Funnel card

- **Invariants**:
  - INV-A-PRESERVATION-COPY-NEVER-REPLACES: a copy kept in `.tc/external/` or `.tc/quarantine/`
    never replaces a file already there; it takes a free name, or finds its own bytes and flushes.
  - INV-EVERY-JOURNAL-FILE-HAS-ONE-STATE: every journal file the session knows has exactly one
    `journal_file::State`, and only `journal_file::next` changes it.
  - INV-FACTS-ARE-PURE: `holds`, `shows_held`, `read_base` and `current_heads` are pure functions
    of the fold state and the ledger's records: no I/O, no Writer session map.
  - INV-ONE-STATE-PER-PATH: a derived path's state lives only in the ledger's `State`; the Writer
    keeps caches only.
  - INV-ATOMIC-WRITES (CHK-1's, extended): raw `Vfs` mutators are callable only from `atomic.rs`.
  - INV-NOTHING-ENTERS-WITHOUT-IMPORT (delta I13, step 9): no derived file's content enters the
    project unless a person chooses *Import* or *Bring it back*.
  - INV-A-COPY-IS-DURABLE-BEFORE-OVERWRITE (step 9; L130, L147): bytes that are not the app's own
    are never written over or removed before their copy and its folder are flushed and the
    manifest names it.
  - INV-EVERY-KEPT-COPY-HAS-ITS-ENTRY (step 9; review R2): each kept copy has its own `kept` entry
    until it is imported or dismissed; a second save never takes over the first's.
  - INV-THE-APPS-OWN-BYTES-ASK-NOTHING (step 9; L146): bytes of `Placed{p}`, of `Writing` `old` or
    `new`, or of the projection never raise a question, a copy or a notice.
