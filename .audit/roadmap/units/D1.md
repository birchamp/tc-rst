# D1: Checkpoints and outside edits

## Goal

Leaving Translate or the project with changes makes one real checkpoint commit, and leaving without
changes makes none (tC4 J8). A failed checkpoint shows "Checkpoint not saved" with Retry on Home
until it is retried, also after a reload. When the project opens or the window gets focus, and
another program changed a book file, the app asks once for every book found: "This book has been
changed externally. Would you like to import it into this project?" with *Import* and *Not now*
(owner ruling, `.audit/roadmap/rulings/2026-10-21-outside-edits.md`, OE 3). The conflict review's
notices list each kept copy with *Import* and *Dismiss* until it is imported or dismissed. A copy
of lost work has *Bring it back*. Another changed generated file shows a one-line notice. There is
no *Undo* of an outside edit, and no *Restore* or *Take in* of a held file: those go
(`docs/STORAGE-DELTA-2026-10-21.md` §3, §6). At quit, the aligner's links are saved before the
checkpoints.

## Why it matters to a translator

The checkpoint is the history Door43 and teammates receive. A translator must know when it was not
saved. A book edited in another program, such as Paratext, must never break the project, be lost,
or enter it without the translator's choice. One question and one click bring it in.

## Funnel card

- **Invariants**:
  - INV-A-LEAVE-WITH-CHANGES-MAKES-ONE-COMMIT: leaving Translate or the project with changes since
    the last commit makes exactly one commit, whose message starts `Checkpoint, leaving Translate`
    or `Checkpoint, leaving the project` (`crates/tc-store/src/checkpoint.rs:375` on the S2 branch);
    leaving without changes, or a save, makes none.
  - INV-A-FAILED-CHECKPOINT-IS-SHOWN-UNTIL-RETRIED: a `Failed` outcome shows on Home and in the save
    indicator until a later checkpoint of that project ends `Committed` or `Unchanged`; `hello`
    lists it, so a reload or a `Resync` shows it again.
  - INV-A-CHANGED-BOOK-IS-ASKED-NOT-TAKEN: a book file another program changed is asked about
    once, in one dialog for every book found together (OE 3.2). Nothing of it enters the project
    until the person chooses *Import*. *Not now* keeps the project's text.
  - INV-A-KEPT-COPY-STAYS-ON-A-SCREEN: every `kept` entry (delta §2.3), a second save's included,
    shows in the conflict review's notices with *Import* and *Dismiss*, or *Bring it back* for
    `why: lost`, until it is imported or dismissed (OE 3.4, review R2). A reload or a `Resync`
    shows it again, because the list comes from the store.
  - INV-QUIT-SAVES-LINKS-THEN-CHECKPOINTS: at quit and at every leave, an open aligner's links are
    saved before the checkpoint runs, so the commit holds them (RT-4).
