# G2: Budgets, whole-Bible runs, Arabic captures and the P7 review

## Goal

The app's budgets (`docs/APP-DESIGN.md` §8.2) are measured on the whole-Bible project by
`appbench` and by Playwright perf specs, nightly in CI, and pass at three times their value. Memory
is measured with a side session. All 62 tC4 baseline screens have an Arabic capture. A person the
owner names has reviewed every listed difference, each residual carries that review's stamp, and
the P7 gate fails a residual without one.

## Why it matters to a translator

Translators keep a whole Bible open for years. This item shows the app stays fast at that size,
and that the Arabic interface looks like tC4's, with every known difference seen by a person.

## Funnel card

- **Invariants**:
  - INV-BUDGETS-AT-3X: every §8.2 budget a benchmark measures is at most three times its value on
    the CI runner, on the whole-Bible project with Psalms in use.
  - INV-EVERY-RESIDUAL-IS-REVIEWED: once `parity/app/units.json` lists G2 as landed, every row and
    general residual in `parity/app/residuals.json` carries `reviewed: { by, date }`, and the P7
    gate fails on one without it.
- **Wrong states**:
  - A screen or action that is fast on Psalms and slow on a whole Bible (tC4 RISKS #3), with no
    check that sees it.
  - Two whole Bibles in memory during a sync from Home beside an open project, over the 350 MiB
    peak (Q-15).
  - Arabic-interface differences from tC4 on 56 screens that nobody has looked at.
  - A residual listed with a reason but never seen by a person (PLAN P7: "A human reviews the
    differences").
- **Code**:
  - Types: `Review { by: String, date: IsoDate }` in `inputs.rs`, with `IsoDate` parsed at the
    boundary (YYYY-MM-DD); field `reviewed: Option<Review>` on `RowResidual` and `GeneralResidual`;
    a new `Rule::Unreviewed` in `gate.rs`.
  - One entry point: the gate's residual check in `gate.rs` is the only place that judges a
    residual; appbench prints perfreport's columns (action, budget, median, p95, verdict) and uses
    its 3x rule, so one rule judges every budget.
  - Removed: nothing.
  - Left to checks, and why a type cannot carry it: speed and memory are facts of a run on a
    machine; a type cannot hold them.
- **Checks**:
  - `every_residual_is_reviewed_once_g2_has_landed` in `crates/tc-ui/tests/screen_record/planted.rs`
    (to create; planted data in memory, like that file's other tests); kind: contract; runs in:
    the CI job that runs `cargo test --workspace`. The real `residuals.json` is checked by the same
    rule on every `cargo test -p tc-ui --test screen_record`.
  - `appbench ci` (to create); kind: count and timing; runs in: P's `perf` job in
    `.github/workflows/ci.yml`.
  - `typing_input_to_paint_p95` (to create) and `align_click_round_trip_p95` (exists) with
    `TC4_PERF=1` on a release UI; kind: journey; runs in: a nightly `perf` job of app-e2e.yml.
  - `first_launch_under_60_s` (to create, five runs, tC4 #751): a measurement on the reference
    laptop through the laptop session, recorded in evidence; it cannot run in CI (Defender and the
    reference machine are the point).
