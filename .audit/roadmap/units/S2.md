## Why it matters to a translator

S2 decides, for every book, alignment and metadata file, whether the bytes on disk are the
app's own or another program's edit. A wrong decision writes over a translator's work or
loses a save after a crash. Sync (X), import (I), checkpoints (D1) and exports (D2) build on it.
