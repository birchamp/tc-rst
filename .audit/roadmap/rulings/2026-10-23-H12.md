# H12: does a Psalm verse whose words sit on poetry lines count as drafted, in Check too?

- Asked: 2026-10-21, `ROADMAP.md` "What needs the owner" (H12), for FIX-R step 4.
- Options: "yes, everywhere" (recommended): Home, the Check rail and the Check screen count a
  verse drafted when its words hold text on any line; or tC4's rule: a verse whose text starts on
  a `\q` line after its `\v` marker is not drafted in Check (tC4 `verseTextIndex`), while Home
  says it is.
- Answer: "poetry - yes, everywhere" (relayed by the orchestrator session on 2026-10-23).

## The rule

One drafted rule everywhere: a verse is drafted when its words, on any line, are neither empty
nor the stub `___` (`tc_app::drafted::words_drafted`). tC4's indexer rule
(`tc_usfm::codec::is_drafted`) no longer decides what Check offers.

## Effect for translators

A Psalm verse written on poetry lines is counted drafted on Home and can be checked and aligned:
Check offers its checks, and a decision on it is saved. tC4 4.0 leaves such verses out of Check,
which this rebuild lists as a deliberate difference.
