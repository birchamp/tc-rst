## The question

D1 makes the toolbar's conflicts button count kept copies too, so a copy left by *Not now* is
always on a screen (INV-A-KEPT-COPY-STAYS-ON-A-SCREEN). With only a kept copy and no verse
conflict, the button reads "1 conflict · Review". Should it say something else, such as "1
change from another program · Review"?

## The ruling

The button keeps tC4's words, `conflicts.reviewOne` / `conflicts.reviewMany`, whatever it counts.
No new words. That the button also counts kept copies stays a declared difference (APP-DESIGN's
D1 row, (f)).
