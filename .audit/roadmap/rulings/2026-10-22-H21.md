# H21: alignment and check-decision files changed by another program

- Asked: 2026-10-22, by the roadmap session, in conversation with the owner.
- Options: A, treat them like other generated files (recommended); B, treat them like book files,
  asked with Import.
- Answer: "agree" (to the recommendation, A).

## The rule

The alignment and check-decision files the app generates are "other generated files" under rule 4
of `2026-10-21-outside-edits.md`. If another program changes one, the app writes it again from its
own record, keeps the changed copy in its store, and shows a one-line notice. No question is
asked and nothing is imported from it. The supported way to bring alignments in from elsewhere is
an aligned USFM file through "Update a book".

## Effect for translators

Nothing is lost; a hand-edited alignment or decision file does not change the project.
