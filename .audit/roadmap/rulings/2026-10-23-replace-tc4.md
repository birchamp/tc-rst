## The rule

The rebuild keeps tC4 4.0's look and behaviour as its baseline. Where tC4 has an open issue or
a planned feature, the rebuild may build it now instead of waiting to copy tC4. REF1 and the
weekly SWEEP still track tC4's fixes. Each such change is a declared difference, recorded where
the design lists them, as `AGENTS.md` "Matching tC4" says.

## Effect for translators

Fixes and features tC4 has planned can reach translators sooner, in the app that will take
tC4's place.
