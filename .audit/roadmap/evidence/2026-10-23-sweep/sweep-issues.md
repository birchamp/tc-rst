## Choices between tC4's behavior and a translator's need that no H question covers

H13 (#822, #813, #821) and H14 (#837) already cover their issues. H7 covers #829 and the tabs of #786/#812. Q30 (a) covers #820 and #825. The one-instance ruling covers #839 and the two-tab case of #802. #830, #831, #832, #833.1 and #836 are bugs that tC4 itself accepts and plans to fix. FIX-R records them as declared differences, so no question is needed. Two issues remain:

1. **Raw `rc://` links in helps (#628, new comment).** BSOJ's Arabic notes for Titus hold a bare `rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-abstractnouns` and a `[[rc://*/ta/man/translate/translate-names]]`. Both are in introductions, the first notes a reader sees. tC4 4.0 and the rebuild show the raw link text. The existing row's "links stay their label until tC4 decides" covers only `[label](href)` links.
   - (a) Match tC4: show the raw text until tC4 decides. Effect: an Arabic reader sees an English URL fragment in the note. Nothing is lost.
   - (b) Show the linked article's title in place of the raw link, with no link behavior. Effect: readable, small change, and a declared difference. tC4 may later choose another form.
   - (c) Make it a working link that opens the article. Effect: most useful, but it is a design that tC4 has not made.
   - Recommendation: (b). It changes only display text, removes raw `rc://` from what a translator reads, and fits whatever tC4 picks later.
2. **Original-language highlight (#840).** With a note current, the Greek and Hebrew panes stay plain, in tC4 4.0 and in the rebuild. The note's quote is in the original language, so the highlight is weakest where a checker needs it most.
   - (a) Wait for tC4 and port it through REF1. Effect: parity is kept, and the translator waits.
   - (b) Build it now from the issue's rule (match `\w` words, note occurrence, a partial match marks nothing). Effect: it helps checkers now, but it is a declared difference and new pixels in the Check and Understand captures.
   - Recommendation: (a). tC4 has filed it with clear acceptance criteria, and it is not a defect against the feature's purpose in 4.0.
