## 1. What B2 is, and what it is not

B2 replaces A4a's stand-in Home (`crates/tc-ui/src/app/web/home_stand_in.rs`, kept by A5 D5) with
the port of tC4's `Home.jsx`, and ports the dialogs Home opens up to their first step. It builds
the server side Home reads: the `home/*` endpoints, `ProjectStore::peek` and `summary_files` on both
backends, the in-memory `SummaryCache` with the `HomeSummary` job, the ordering and the Resume
record, the unusable folders of C0's listing with words for them, the offline language search
(AD27, tC4 #492) and the versification list. It ports tC4's `homeProjectCard` and `homeObsCard`
tests and the UI half of tC4's `language-suggestions` journey, and captures B2's parity rows.

Not B2 (each draws a control whose `Actions` body the named unit writes, the A5 D9 precedent):
opening a project from a tile, a story tile or the Resume card (B3); Settings (B9a); the Share or
Sync flow after its internet question (B9b, B10); the sync result toast (B10); the conflict
review the chip opens (B11, C7a); Create in M2/M3, the pick and USFM steps of M4 (C6); the files,
review and working steps of M5 (F1); Open a project from Door43 (B9b); `home/nameFree` (moved to
