## Steps (each one commit, built and tested)

1. **tc-helps block renderer.** `tc_helps::blocks`: port of tC4's `renderArticleBlocks` and
   `takeSpans` (`tC4:src/data/articles.ts:100-175`), with tC4's own cases as tests (#619).
2. **tc-api.** `session/open` (job, `JobOutcome::Opened`), `session/close`; `editor/book`,
   `editor/sources`; `helps/passage`, `helps/article`; the view types (`BookView`,
   `SourcePane`, `Slot`, `ArticleBlock`, cards with per-pane highlight and title); `model`
   with `take_spans`; `HoldPoint::Open`; goldens.
3. **tc-app.** The open job over `SessionRegistry::open_screen` (cancel the Home summary, mark
   the project used, the remembered place and draft unit in the outcome), `session/close`
   through `leave_screen`; the book read (rail from the Home summaries, chapters, the book's
   comments); `BookResources` per session (current and previous book) through
   `tc_resources::load_book`, counted as `book_resource_loads`; the source panes with section
   starts, paragraph marks and tokens; helps per book with each card's gateway title and lit
   tokens per pane (`tc_helps::highlight`); articles. Tests through `App::request` on the
   `sample` and `en-pins` scenarios; count rows `open_book_touches_one_book` and
   `helps_load_one_book`; the `take_spans` differential test (tc-api's model against
   tc-helps' renderer on every Titus note at every length).
4. **tc-ui.** Pure models on the host (open overlay decision with its own timer, Understand
   units, section starts, paragraphs, focus, preview cut, comment selection rule); views
   `open_progress`, `understand`, `book_rail`, `rail_chrome`, `panel_icons`, `source_verse`,
   `helps_panel`; the actions (open from a tile and from Resume, refused open on Home, close at
   Home, book and chapter, source tab, helps tab, focus, divider). The comment box shows the
   comment and is read-only until C3 writes it, so nothing typed can be lost.
5. **Parity.** J15 journey (held open, overlay at 300 ms with no frame), Understand journeys,
   the four Understand screens captured, `units.json` gains B3, report reviewed, residuals
   written.
6. **Docs.** APP-DESIGN B3 notes (D-n) where a decision was made, Q-7's mapping, TC4-ISSUES
   rows for B3.

## Rules kept

- Nothing writes the project; the comment box cannot drop typed text (read-only until C3).
- A wrong verse numbering is never shown: a project whose versification is not the source's
  shows tC4's "frame unavailable" statement in place of a passage until the mapping is built.
- Every count test runs on Psalms and ten times Psalms.
