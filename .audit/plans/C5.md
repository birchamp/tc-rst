## Applied C5 continuation

1. **Recover unsaved links.** Actor edits now submit drafts with both displayed tokens. A
   newer differing durable draft survives an older successful save before the actor's Ack
   (R26), and a failed final quit leaves recoverable links. Ack may refresh draft tokens;
   retaining the newer links does not depend on it. The existing save model now separates
   durable store completion from actor Ack and seeds the original loss back into its proof.
2. **Learn confirmed saves.** Background work receives the exact saved version, not newer
   unsaved links. Re-saves are not counted twice. Growth thresholds trigger retraining;
   snapshot generations and cancellation fence late results, and saves arriving during
   initial training or retraining remain learned. Testament models stay separate.
3. **Keep queued actions through refresh and close.** Suggestion refresh preserves pending
   clicks and the workspace. Closing drains queued actions before saving. Transport failure
   retains the exact request and server run for Retry; typed refusal retains the actions and
   blocks navigation. An older Saved flush reply cannot release a newer completed edit.
4. **Bound interactive work and prediction cost.** The event pump resolves this device as
   Mine directly instead of reading every device's profile on each local save. Alignment
   saves now have strict Psalms/10× counts, including while training holds its captured
   snapshot. Profiled prediction changes compile fitted trees into contiguous nodes, score
   bounded batches in the original addition order, and derive sentence-only n-gram metrics
   once. No prediction cache, counter limit or timing budget was introduced or relaxed.
