## 1. Summary

**The reference stays at d310bf3.** tC4 cut rc.2 from `main` at b1d1f37. Every user-visible
change below is in rc.2 except the licence change (#838), which is on both branches but
after the tag.

**What changed for translators in tC4.**

- Align: the word bank holds the words the export checks (#809, #811). One "needs review"
  rule serves the rail, the notice, Mark valid, the tC3 import and the export (#814). The
  export leaves out a verse that needs review and names it. A link follows its own word
  after a Translate edit; an unclear edit marks the verse for review (#815, #824). Source
  cards are repaired from the pinned verse (#816). Hebrew source words are compared in one
  Unicode form (#805). A verse that already holds alignment markup does not open (#799).
  A verse whose text starts on the line after its number cannot be aligned (#800).
- Translate: a poetry verse is one body over all its lines, shown as tabs; a `\p` or `\b`
  in mid-verse no longer cuts the verse (#810). A `\v` right after `\q1` is a verse (#785).
  The space after a footnote stays (#800).
- Sync: the result names each verse where a teammate's alignment replaced yours (#806).
  After a sync, a refused save shows one plain sentence and Home leaves (#803, #808).
- Import: a project with no versification file imports with the English scheme (#817).
- About: "License: MIT" (#838).

**The rebuild already does most of this.** The fixes of FIX-align and FIX-text cover #782,
#787, #789, #790, #793, #794, #795 and #796, and their rows now say covered. Where tC4 and
the rebuild still differ, the rebuild serves the translator better or the owner is asked
(§5).

**Five new rebuild bugs**, from tC4 issues, all read from code and all FIX-R's:
#825 (a link sent back to the bank is not listed), #830 (placed words on an Old Testament
card read backwards), #832 (Home ignores a direction changed in Settings), #833.1 (a raw
error when checking for updates offline), #836 (English helps read right to left under an
Arabic set). `TC4-ISSUES.md` §1 rows 18 to 22.

## 5. Decisions for the project owner

### Questions (asked 2026-10-23)

**Q37. Does an edit in Align clear "needs review"? (tC4 #814, D105 point 5)**
A verse is marked "needs review" after a source text upgrade or a teammate's change. In tC4,
only Mark valid clears the mark. In the rebuild, moving one word clears it, even if the rest
of the verse was never looked at.

- (a) Follow tC4: only Mark valid clears it. The verse stays red until the translator says it
  is right.
- (b) Keep the rebuild's rule: the first edit clears it.
- Recommendation: (a).

Answer: (a), "yes, needs-review" (2026-10-23; `rulings/2026-10-23-Q37-needs-review.md`).

**Q38. Raw `rc://` links in helps (tC4 #628).**
Some notes hold a link written as `rc://…` (for example BSOJ's Arabic notes for Titus). tC4 and
the rebuild show the raw link text, an English address in the middle of an Arabic note.

- (a) Match tC4: show the raw text until tC4 decides.
- (b) Show the linked article's title as plain text, with no link behavior.
- (c) Make it a working link that opens the article.
- Recommendation: (b). It is readable, small, and fits whatever tC4 picks later.

Answer: (a), "raw links are fine" (2026-10-23; `rulings/2026-10-23-Q38-raw-rc-links.md`).

**Q39. Highlight the note's words in the Greek or Hebrew text (tC4 #840).**
When a note is open, tC4 and the rebuild highlight its words only in the gateway text. The
original-language text stays plain.

- (a) Wait for tC4 to build it, then copy it (through REF1).
- (b) Build it now from the issue's rule.
- Recommendation: (a).

Answer: (b), "build it" (2026-10-23; `rulings/2026-10-23-Q39-original-language-highlight.md`); card OLH-1.
