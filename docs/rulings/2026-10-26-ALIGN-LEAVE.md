# ALIGN-LEAVE: Home with an aligner click that was not saved

- Asked: 2026-10-26, by the E1d lane through the orchestrator session, after E1d.
- Answer (relayed by the orchestrator, 2026-10-26): option 1, "Home asks: Stay / Leave without
  it", with the owner's words: "1, but wait until you're sure that the question needs to be
  asked. I don't want them to be asked if it's going to be saved in a second or two."

## The question

A click in the aligner that the server refused, or that never reached it, lives only in the
page. Home kept the person in the aligner with no way out. Should Home ask, drop the click
silently, or keep holding?

## The ruling

Home asks Stay or Leave without it, and only once the click is lost for sure: the server
refused it for good, or it is still unsaved after a short, fixed limit. While the click is on
its way or being sent again, Home waits without a word and leaves as soon as it saves. Leave
drops only the clicks that could not be saved.
