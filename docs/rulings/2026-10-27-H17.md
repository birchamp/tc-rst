# H17: measure the speed budgets, don't block on them

- Asked: 2026-10-22, `ROADMAP.md` "What needs the owner" (H17): are the speed budgets part of
  done? Put to the owner again on 2026-10-27 by REL-1.
- Answer: 2026-10-27, relayed by the roadmap session: "Measure, don't block."

## The rule

1. Every budget row of `docs/PERFORMANCE.md` is measured on the reference laptop, in a release
   build, and the numbers are recorded in `docs/evidence/`.
2. 4.1.0 does not wait for the budgets to be met.
3. Each row that misses its budget becomes a card.
4. Operation-count tests stay in the gate. An interactive action still costs what it touches,
   never the size of the project.

## Change to AGENTS.md

AGENTS.md "Performance" ends "and meet the budgets in release builds." By this ruling it should
read: "Measure the budgets in release builds on the reference laptop and record them in
`docs/evidence/`; a row that misses becomes a card (ruling H17)."

## Effect for translators

A slow spot may reach testers first. It is measured and named, and each one has a card.

## Unblocks and changes

REL-1 and DONE no longer wait for the budgets. The measurement on the reference laptop is still
owed: it is a card of its own.
